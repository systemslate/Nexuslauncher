Setups

Share the home screen you built.

A setup is a picture of a home screen. Share your setup makes that picture. First launch, permissions and the settings tour live in the user guide.

In the app

How Share your setup works.

Open Settings › Share your setup. The subtitle in the app is “A picture of your home screen to share.”

  1. Pick the picture

    Use the live home screen, or choose Use custom setup image when you already have a shot.

  2. Frame it

    Orientation is Portrait or Landscape. Frame is None, Rounded, Border or Phone. You can also set the background, corner radius, frame color and the Nexus mark.

  3. Save or share

    Save image writes a PNG through the system file picker. Share opens the Android share sheet.
