Prefer email? Write to systemslate.dev@gmail.com — screenshots and crash logs are welcome there too.

Answers are stored with the existing Google Form, so Google’s privacy policy still applies. The original form stays available if this one will not send.

What the form asks

  • The kind of feedback — bug, feature request or general.
  • Which area it is about, such as the drawer, dock or widgets.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • The steps that reproduce it, and how often it happens.
  • Device model, Android version and Nexus version.
  • An email address, only if you want a reply.

Crash logs

After a crash, open Settings › Feedback › Crash logs. Copy the entry, or use the share action to send it as plain text. Logs stay on your device until you choose to send one.

Before you write

The user guide covers every screen and has a troubleshooting table for common problems. It may answer the question faster.

Other routes

Where each kind of message belongs.

Play Store reviews are useful but hard to reply to. These channels get a response.

Bug report

Something behaves differently from what the guide describes. Include the steps that reproduce it.

Feature request

Describe the problem you are trying to solve, not only the setting you want. That makes the request far easier to act on.

Privacy question

Anything unclear in the privacy policy — ask directly and you will get a plain answer.

Translation fix

Nexus ships in 10 languages. If a string reads wrong in yours, send the language and the screen it appears on.
