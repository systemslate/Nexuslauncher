The short version

Nexus Launcher does not collect your personal data on any server, does not show ads, and does not sell or share your data.

1. Data stays on your device

Your home screen layout, folders, Canvas designs, wallpapers, themes, icon choices, notes, settings and app usage data are stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that receive this information, and the app has no accounts or sign-in.

2. What the app accesses and why

Depending on which features you use, the app may ask for the following. Each is optional unless noted, and each is used only on your device to provide the feature described.

  • Installed apps list (required for a launcher): to show your apps in the drawer, dock and home screen, to search them, and to sort them into categories.
  • Notification access: to show unread badges on app icons and to show and control what is playing in the music widget. Notification contents are not stored or transmitted.
  • Usage access: to sort apps by how often you use them and to show app usage in the battery widget. Processed on your device only.
  • Accessibility service: only if you turn it on. It is used for two things: home screen gestures (opening notifications, quick settings, recent apps or split screen, showing the power menu, taking a screenshot), and, only if you turn on Strict Lock Mode, checking which app is open so locked apps ask you to unlock them. It does not read what is on your screen, and nothing it sees leaves your device.
  • Location (approximate): only if you use the weather widget. Your approximate location is sent to the weather service (see section 3) to get the forecast for where you are. On some Android versions, location permission is also what lets the status row show your Wi-Fi network name; that name is never transmitted.
  • Contacts: only if you turn on contact search. Contacts are read when you search and are not stored or transmitted.
  • Calendar: only if you add a calendar or agenda widget. Events are read to show them and are not stored or transmitted.
  • Photos: only when you choose a picture, for example for a wallpaper, icon or widget. Pictures are chosen through Android's photo picker, so the app only sees the ones you pick.
  • Screen capture: only if you use frosted-glass effects and Android cannot give the app your wallpaper directly (for example with a live wallpaper). After you approve Android's screen capture prompt, the app captures one image of your wallpaper to build the blur, then stops. The image stays on your device.
  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth: only for the Wi-Fi and Bluetooth shortcuts and status indicators you add.
  • Biometrics: only if you use app lock. Authentication is handled by Android; the app never receives your fingerprint or face data.

3. Internet access

The app uses the internet only for features you use:

  • Weather: requests go to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) with your approximate location. Open-Meteo's privacy policy applies to those requests.
  • News feeds and reader: the app downloads the news feeds you add, and the articles and images you open, directly from those websites.
  • Web search: searches you send to the web open in your browser or search app.

These requests go straight from your device to that service. They are subject to that service's own privacy policy, and they do not include any information about you beyond what the request itself needs.

4. Crash reports

If the app crashes, a crash log is saved on your device (Settings › Feedback › Crash logs). It contains the time, the app version, your device model and Android version, and technical details of the error. It does not contain your personal data, and it is never sent automatically. If you choose to report a crash, the app opens your own email app with the log attached, and nothing is sent unless you send that email.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

6. Purchases

If you buy a Premium upgrade, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase is valid.

7. Backups

If you make a backup, the file is saved wherever you choose with Android's file picker. If you choose a cloud location there (for example Google Drive), the file is stored by that provider under your own account. We never receive your backups.

8. Children

Nexus Launcher is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Your choices

You can turn off any permission at any time in Android Settings, or uninstall the app to remove all data it stored on your device.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
